# Canary support

Canary helps people notice sensitive content and review its label in Office or Outlook. Its desktop review runs locally on Mac and Windows.

[Get started](https://canarylabels.com/support) · [Check compatibility](https://canarylabels.com/compatibility) · [Configure Canary Enterprise](https://canarylabels.com/enterprise)

## Bugs and questions

[Search existing issues](https://github.com/clarkdickens/canary-support/issues) or [open a bug report or question](https://github.com/clarkdickens/canary-support/issues/new/choose). Issues are public. Describe the problem with made-up text and filenames. Keep real documents, email, screenshots containing personal information, and company policy files on your device.

## Security vulnerabilities

[Report a vulnerability privately](https://github.com/clarkdickens/canary-support/security/advisories/new). Include the affected version and steps to reproduce with fictional data.
